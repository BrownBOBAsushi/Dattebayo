# Multiplayer lightning artwork

`lightning-frame.png` and `lightning-frame-02.png` were generated on 2026-09-13 with the built-in image-generation tool for the multiplayer lightning effect. Each is a distinct 2171 × 724 RGBA pose with branching blue-white arcs and a transparent centre. The generated pixels and alpha are preserved; browser code supplies the changing poses, charge glow and confirmed impact timing.

Visual direction came from the user's attached lightning-border reference associated with [this Pinterest pin](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/948359634017880687/). The pin could not be retrieved. These assets were generated from the written prompts below; the reference image was not copied into the project.

## Pose A prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original production game VFX overlay, isolated cinematic realistic lightning border. Generate a NEW very wide horizontal 3:1 image, approximately 2172x724, with a TRUE TRANSPARENT RGBA background. Draw only electric arcs around the outer perimeter of a rectangular arena: two shallow irregular lightning networks hugging the top and bottom edges, connected by thinner broken branching bolts at the left and right edges. Photorealistic electrical discharge, needle-fine dendritic forks, naturally uneven jagged main channels, brilliant thin icy-white cores, cobalt and electric-blue soft corona, sparse tiny blue sparks at the edges. Not cartoon, not pixel art, not graphic line art. Intensely cinematic but controlled bloom, fine sharp filament detail and plausible branching scales. Keep the central 80 percent of the width and central 62 percent of the height completely empty and transparent so game fighters remain visible. Main bolts remain within the outer 15 percent height at top and bottom and outer 9 percent width at the sides; no long bolts across the center. The composition must form an organic open border, not a solid decorative frame. Soft outer light falloff must be encoded as transparency. NO background whatsoever, NO checkerboard or transparency preview pattern, NO black/blue/white/gray field, NO fog or cloud behind the lightning, NO scene, NO characters, NO text, NO watermark, NO logos, NO UI. Render the lightning only and leave all other pixels genuinely transparent. One finished isolated border asset. Animation pose A: a strong forked zigzag runs along the upper left into a finer horizontal upper band; the upper right has a compact branching knot. Along the lower band two thin main arcs curl around separate bright junctions near the lower quarter positions, with downward/outward tiny forks. Keep brightness reasonably even around the full perimeter.
